## 6. Four buckets, plus quote-candidates tracked separately

**Bucket definitions used in this pass** (not literally specified in the task brief — constructed here, stated explicitly so the mapping can be checked/overridden rather than trusted blind):

- **Pass both** — closeness `PASS` and citation `pass` or `no_references` (nothing to fix or verify further).
- **Needs fixing** — closeness `PASS` (wording is fine) but citation `fail` (has a specific unverified reference). Remediation is narrow and already designed: strip the one ungrounded reference span, per Invariant 11 / the Part 1 prevention fix's own boundary-safe-strip design — nothing else about the statement is implicated.
- **Needs removing** — closeness `HOLD_TOO_LITTLE` (residual content is too little to be a real distinct statement on its own — a near-bare citation) *and* citation `fail`. Nothing salvageable by a strip: strip the bad reference and what's left is sub-threshold filler. **0 statements landed here this pass.**
- **Needs manual review** — citation `uncertain` (can't determine groundedness either way — never auto-act per the check's own fail-toward-UNCERTAIN design), *or* closeness `HOLD_TOO_LITTLE` with a citation that isn't itself a confirmed failure (too little content to judge the wording call, needs a human read).
