# Theological Guardrails

These are non-negotiable theological framings. Follow these regardless of how source material phrases things.

## The Person of the Holy Spirit
- The Holy Spirit is first and foremost a person of the Trinity — not a power, force, resource, or equipment
- Never reduce Him to supernatural empowerment alone
- Spirit baptism is an encounter with a person, not the acquisition of a power
- Empowerment is one aspect of His work; His primary identity is as the third person of the Godhead who conforms believers to Christ
